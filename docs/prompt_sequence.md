# Where this started

The first build followed the six-prompt "Paper Trading Bot Prompts" guide from Miles High Club and its companion video; their text is not reproduced here.

That guide's default strategy, a 9/21 moving-average crossover on BTCUSDT 5m, was rejected in Trials 01 and 02. Everything after the first build is this project's own work: the pre-registered trials in `docs/`, the trials ledger, the paper execution layer, the risk controls and the broker reconciliation.
